Dry filament prints cleaner.

Filament picks up moisture from the air, which can lead to stringing, popping and weak layers. Each NH Fill box includes a silica gel pack that sits with the spool and soaks up humidity while it's stored.

  • Silica gel pack in every box, beside the spool
  • Clip-on lid keeps the spool closed between prints
  • Keep the pack in the box after you start the spool
  • Silica gel is not food: keep it away from children and pets
